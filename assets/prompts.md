# Image-2 素材生成提示词

## hero-logistics.png

Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: website hero background for an express logistics tracking web page
Primary request: A polished modern logistics operations scene for a Chinese express delivery tracking interface, showing delivery vans, parcel boxes, route lines, and a clean warehouse dispatch area.
Style/medium: high-end semi-realistic 3D illustration, web hero asset
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition, central subject slightly right, usable clean negative space on the left for UI copy; no UI screens or readable text in the image
Lighting/mood: bright daytime, efficient, reliable, professional
Color palette: crisp white, teal, graphite, amber accents; avoid dominant purple, beige, dark navy, or brown palette
Constraints: no brand logos, no readable text, no watermark, no distorted people, no real company marks

## parcel-scan.png

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: supporting image card for express parcel tracking website
Primary request: Clean close-up scene of sorted cardboard parcels with a handheld barcode scanner light and subtle delivery route graphic overlay.
Style/medium: high-end semi-realistic 3D product illustration, web asset
Composition/framing: square image, parcel stack and scanner arranged diagonally, plenty of clean edge padding
Lighting/mood: bright studio logistics environment, precise, trustworthy
Color palette: cardboard neutrals balanced with teal, graphite, white, and small amber accents; do not let beige dominate
Constraints: no brand logos, no readable labels, no watermark, no human faces, no company marks
